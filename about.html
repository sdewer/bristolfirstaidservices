Who We Are

About Bristol First Aid Services

Bristol First Aid Services offers first aid training for businesses, schools and individuals across the Bristol region. We think knowing how to respond in an emergency should be as common as knowing how to call 999.

All training is delivered by Phil, a working Paramedic with years of front-line emergency experience across Bristol and Somerset. He teaches the same skills he uses on the job, so the training is grounded in what actually happens rather than what textbooks say should.

Courses are available for workplaces of all sizes and risk levels, from a half-day introduction through to the full three-day First Aid at Work qualification. We also offer paediatric training and CPR and defibrillator sessions.

We are based in Bristol and cover Bath, Weston-super-Mare, North Somerset and the surrounding area. All training takes place at your premises, so there is no need to travel or hire a venue.

Phil

Lead Trainer & Qualified Paramedic

Phil is a working Paramedic who has responded to emergencies across Bristol and Somerset for a number of years. He runs Bristol First Aid Services alongside his clinical work, which means the training stays current and grounded in real experience.

He started Bristol First Aid Services because he saw, first-hand, how often bystanders froze in an emergency. Good training changes that.
