Course Overview

The Level 3 First Aid at Work is the full workplace first aid qualification. Over three days, delegates work through a broad range of emergency scenarios with plenty of hands-on practice time. It is required by the HSE for high-risk workplaces including construction, manufacturing and engineering.

What You’ll Learn

  • First aider roles and legal responsibilities
  • CPR for adults, children and infants
  • AED use with hands-on practice
  • Airway management and rescue breathing
  • Severe bleeding control including tourniquet use
  • Shock: recognition and management
  • Major trauma including head, chest and abdominal injuries
  • Fractures, spinal injuries and safe casualty handling
  • Anaphylaxis and auto-injector use
  • Cardiac events, strokes and diabetic emergencies
  • Asthma and other respiratory emergencies
  • Multi-casualty scenario practice

Who Is It For?

  • High-risk workplaces: construction, manufacturing, engineering and healthcare support
  • Organisations where the HSE requires a qualified first aider on site
  • Anyone whose Level 3 FAW certificate has expired and needs to be renewed

Frequently Asked Questions

How long is the Level 3 First Aid at Work course?

It's a full three-day course covering CPR, trauma, medical emergencies, fractures, burns and more.

Which workplaces need Level 3?

Level 3 is required by the HSE for higher-risk workplaces such as construction sites, factories and engineering workshops.

How long is the certificate valid?

Your Level 3 First Aid at Work certificate is valid for 3 years.

Is the course delivered at our site?

Yes, training takes place on-site at your premises, with groups of up to 12 delegates.
