Course Overview

Children respond to emergencies differently from adults, and that changes what a first aider needs to do. This two-day course is designed for anyone working with infants and children who needs a solid, practical grounding in paediatric first aid.

Our trainer’s paramedic clinical background means paediatric emergencies are understood at a level beyond a standard trainer, giving delegates exceptional insight into why children respond differently and what that means in practice.

The course meets Ofsted requirements and EYFS framework standards for childcare providers.

What You’ll Learn

  • CPR for infants and children
  • AED use for children
  • Choking in infants and children
  • Unconsciousness and the recovery position
  • Febrile convulsions and seizures
  • Anaphylaxis and auto-injector use
  • Asthma and breathing difficulties
  • Head injuries, falls and fractures
  • Burns and scalds
  • Meningitis: signs and response
  • Poisoning and swallowed objects
  • Basic safeguarding awareness

Who Is It For?

  • Nursery and early years staff (required by Ofsted)
  • Childminders and nannies
  • School staff and teaching assistants
  • After-school and holiday club staff
  • Parents or carers who want to be better prepared
